EE Design

This document holds the electrical design of the robot arm: the board and cable defs of the EE model, how they refine the system model, and an overview of the boards and harnesses. The EE requirements are in the EE Requirements Specification (ee-req), the EE tests in ee-test-spec and ee-test-report (V-model split, tiacsys/xdmod#69 decision Q8). Each board has its own document (pcba-<board>, in docs/pcbas/); every cable is in the document harnesses.

Note

This is a skeleton. The content comes in Phase 6.

What will go here

  • the design decisions that span boards and harnesses (supply, buses, connectors), with the EE requirements each one meets;

  • the wiring of each prototype setup (NUCLEO-G474RE board, breakout boards, servos and potentiometers), or a link to its proto-<name> document.

The boards and their connector ports are defs in the EE model (model/), which refines the system model by specialization (XD-ADR-0130). Their needs and links come from the model.

How the EE model refines the system model: each board and harness realizes a system element, connector ports conform to system interfaces (none on the canary), and a board conforms to its space budget. The lines are the trace links of the needs below (XD-ADR-0132):

EE-PORT-0002 (the plug of the bought test lead) has no board and conforms to no system interface; only the cable connects it.
