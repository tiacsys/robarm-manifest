Time grows with the drawn pixels and the facet count: 4 to 8 s per view<br/>for 272 000 facets (60 filleted tubes), about 1 s per view for a few boxes, on the owner’s machine,<br/>well inside the `freecad` limit of 10 minutes for a few variants. |
