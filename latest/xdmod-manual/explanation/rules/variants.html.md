(XD-RULE-0021)*](#XD-RULE-0021), [*Feature constraints hold (XD-RULE-0022)*](#XD-RULE-0022) and [*One product model per confi... (XD-RULE-0023)*](#XD-RULE-0023) to [*Exactly one variant per var... (XD-RULE-0027)*](#XD-RULE-0027).
