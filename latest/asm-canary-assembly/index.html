Canary Assembly (asm-canary-assembly)

This document describes the assembly canary-assembly (ME-ASM-0001): the FreeCAD top document assemblies/canary-assembly/canary-assembly.FCStd, its skeleton canary-assembly-skeleton.FCStd and the assembly def of the ME model it implements. It was made by curatrix new asm; every assembly document has the same sections (ME-4, XD-ADR-0129). Parameters, materials, interfaces and the BOM come from the FreeCAD files and the ME model in plain Python; drawings, exploded view, renderings, mass and findings come from the local curatrix cam output of the current commit and are not part of CI builds.

Overview

  • Assembly: ME-ASM-0001, FreeCAD document assemblies/canary-assembly/canary-assembly.FCStd

  • Skeleton: assemblies/canary-assembly/canary-assembly-skeleton.FCStd

  • Version, drawings, mass, findings: Made locally with FreeCAD (curatrix cam) for the current commit; not part of this build.

  • Variants: none (no link has Configs)

  • Sub-assemblies: none

  • Parts below it (links, all levels): 4
