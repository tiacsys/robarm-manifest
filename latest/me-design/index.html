ME Requirements and Design

This document holds the mechanical requirements of the robot arm and the mechanical design that meets them.

Note

This is a skeleton. The content comes in Phase 6.

What will go here

  • the ME requirements, written as EARS sentences, each refining a system requirement;

  • the assemblies and parts, with their parameters (read from the FreeCAD spreadsheets), drawings and 3D views;

  • the ME test cases and their results.

In iteration 1 this is one document. It will be split into a requirements document, an assembly design document and test documents once that content exists.
